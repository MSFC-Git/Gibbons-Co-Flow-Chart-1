How a project budget moves from Excel into WorkflowMax, is costed against as work happens, and becomes the base for reporting. Select any step for details, or walk through it in order.

Overview

Five stages, one source of truth

The budget is built and refined in Excel, uploaded once to WorkflowMax, then costed against through purchase orders, payments and variations. Because every cost sits against a budget line, the result can be reported on.
