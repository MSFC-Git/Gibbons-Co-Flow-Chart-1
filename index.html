From Budget To Reporting

Excel is great for building a budget. It's not the place to run one. Here's how a project budget moves from Excel into WorkflowMax, gets costed against as work happens, and becomes the base for reporting. Pick any step for the detail, or walk through it in order.

Overview

Five stages, one source of truth

The budget is built and refined in Excel, uploaded once to WorkflowMax, then costed against through purchase orders, payments and variations. Because every cost sits against a budget line, the result can be reported on.
